# My contribution and AI assistance

I am Dhairya Lukha. Wallpaper Hub is my personal project, developed with AI assistance.

## My role

- Choosing the project direction and the interaction I wanted.
- Reviewing generated artwork and the behaviour of the experience.
- Requesting corrections when the results did not match the intended appearance or interaction.
- Comparing iterations and deciding which directions to keep, defer or reject.

The project records include corrections to artwork identity, reveal behaviour, trail appearance and interface behaviour. Some experiments were rejected rather than presented as finished features.

## AI's role

AI tools assisted with source-code generation, technical implementation, documentation, artwork generation and iterative changes. The website was developed in a Claude-assisted workspace; the wider project also used other AI-assisted development workflows.

Repository ownership and commits should not be interpreted as proof that I manually authored every line. I do not present this project as evidence of independent professional programming or manual illustration proficiency.

## What the project demonstrates

The useful evidence is the process: turning an idea into specific requirements, communicating those requirements to AI tools, inspecting outputs, identifying problems and requesting further iterations. Technical test results are reported separately from my visual judgments and from commercial-release readiness.
